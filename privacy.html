Meal Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Meal Tracker is a personal meal-logging web app (a fork of FitScan). It is operated by a single individual for their own use and has no server of its own.

What stays on your device

Your diary, goals, custom foods and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. The developer cannot see them.

Google Drive access

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests access to your Google Drive so it can read and write the markdown files inside the folder AI Memory/Meal Tracker (daily meal files and a settings file). It reads and writes only those files. The sign-in token is kept in memory in your browser and is not stored or sent anywhere other than to Google's APIs.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide the meal-tracking and sync features, is not shared with third parties, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Other services

Looking up foods sends the barcode or search text to Open Food Facts and, if you add your own API key, to USDA FoodData Central. No account details are sent.

No tracking, no sale of data

The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer does not collect, sell or share personal data.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete local data from the app's Settings. Files in your Drive remain yours and can be deleted there.
